# Rise to Ruin: the player's backstory

Why the player wants to corrupt and humiliate his mother and sister, and why he hands them to other men. Agreed with the author on 2026-11-25. This is the reference for every scene that shows his darker side; [LATER_STAGES_VISION.md](LATER_STAGES_VISION.md) builds on it.

**Names:** design doc, so role labels (Mom, Sister, Dad, Boss, Best Friend). In the game every one of them is a variable.

---

## 1. The core

The career failure explains why he wants power. It doesn't explain why he wants it over *them*. That comes from home.

**He loves them, and he keeps score.** The warmth is real, which is why he can be a friendly son and a joking brother day to day (`CHARACTER_VOICES.md`). Underneath it is a ledger, and in the key scenes the ledger shows.

**The rules of the house.** The family has always run on two currencies:
- **Being liked.** That's Sister: popular, easy, the middle of every photo. Things come to her.
- **Being useful.** That's Mom: everyone's helper, loved for what she does for people.
- He has never been much of either, so he got the leftovers, warmly, with a hug.
- Sister says the rule out loud in More Time Needed, as a joke: *"You make yourself useful and people are nice to you. Welcome home."*

**The third rule, from Dad:** the one who withholds holds all the power.

Everything he does later turns these rules around: now *they* have to be useful to be loved, now *they* stand at the edge, and he's the one who decides when the warmth comes back.

## 2. The wounds

### Dad: the lesson
- The spring of the player's senior year, a few weeks after he turned eighteen, Dad stopped talking to Mom. No yelling, he never yelled. One-word answers, dinner with his phone, and he let her try: she cooked his chili, bought the beer he liked, laughed too loud at the TV so he'd look over.
- After six weeks he left. Mom sat at the kitchen table with the player until two in the morning, asking what she'd done.
- What he learned: Dad held all the power in that house for six weeks by doing nothing, and Mom would have done anything to get the warmth back.
- For a few months after that the player was the one she leaned on, the man of the house. Then she got back on her feet, cheerfully helped him pack when he moved out, and he was "sweetie" again.
- Dad called Sister on her birthday. He didn't call the player on his.
- **He knows he's copying Dad, and he's quietly proud that he does it better.** Dad needed six weeks and still had to leave to make his point. The player needs one week and gets to stay. (The Silence, The Crawl.)

### Mom: she gives in to power, and she chose someone else
1. **The garage.** Right after he founded the LLC he picked her up from work. She came down to the parking garage with her Boss, and she introduced him: "my son, he's got a little business going". The Boss asked what he was selling, lemonade? She laughed along, because that's how she gets through her days with that man. In the car she said "You have to pick your battles, honey." The Boss doesn't remember him in Stage 0 ("I don't remember booking a meeting with a teenager"); she does.
2. **The loan.** When the café dropped him he asked her for $8,000 to keep the servers running for three months. She was lovely about it: tea, his hand held across the kitchen table, and the explanation that Sister's tuition was due and maybe this was a sign to try something steadier for a while, sweetie. She meant it kindly, and that's the part he can't get over.

**What he wants:** to be the power she gives in to, and to take away the respectability she used to look down on him with, kindly. That's the vision's "Mom loses respectability" (1.5), and the reason floor 6 in front of her old colleagues has to hurt.

### Sister: private love, public distance
- At home she was his best friend. They've been close since they were kids, and that was always true.
- At school (she's a year older) she had a reputation to look after, and he wasn't part of it. In his junior year he and his school-project partner showed their headset at the science fair, and by Monday her friends called it **"the creep helmet"**, because it listened to rooms. They made a beeping noise whenever he walked past their lockers. She laughed along. Telling them to stop would have cost her something, so she never did. That same evening she sat on his bed and asked him to show her how it worked, and was really impressed. He never understood how both could be true.
- In college she told the story to her new friends. The first time Best Friend came over, she asked across the kitchen whether he was "the creep helmet guy", and Sister laughed and said yes.
- She has forgotten all of it. Then she asks for a demo of that same device for her class project, and her idea gets him the diner.

**What he wants:** back then she wouldn't spend an ounce of her popularity on him. Now she spends all of it, because he asks. That's the vision's "Sister loses status, her lever is devotion" (1.5). The Friend is the first payment: she mutes the group chat with the girl who called him the creep helmet guy.

### The other men
Every venue owner is someone who looked down on him, and his family deferred to those men instead of standing up for him: the Boss (Mom laughed along), the Manager (mocked him across the fryer), the Landlord (the broom closet), the Prof, the college athletes (the kind of guys who ran with the creep helmet). Handing Mom and Sister to them is profit, and it's a verdict: *"You always took their side. Go on, then."* It also makes those men need him. He tells himself it's business, sometimes revenge. Really it's about never standing at the edge again.

### The photo
A photo in the upstairs hallway from Sister's high school graduation: Dad's arm around her, Mom laughing at something off camera, and the player on the end, half cut off by the frame. Nobody did it on purpose, and nobody offered to take another one. It's the image behind "In every group photo from school I'm standing at the edge." Later callbacks: the Souvenirs party photos, the date photo, and eventually a new family photo with him in the middle.
